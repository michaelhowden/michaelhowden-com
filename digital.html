Digital

I’ve spent my career designing and leading information systems, from open-source disaster management software used in Asia, the Pacific and the US, to regulatory platforms, data governance and environmental data science in New Zealand. Along the way I’ve become more interested in where digital tools don’t help, especially when the system is social rather than physical.
